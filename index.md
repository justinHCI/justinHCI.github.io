    <h2>About Me</h2>
    <p>I am an Assistant Professor in the Digital Studies program in Stockton University's School of Arts and Humanities. I also hold an affiliat possition as a visiting research fellow to the Faculty of Education and Psychology at the University of Oulu. I investigate how people speak to one another and to machines.</p>
        
    <p> My research focuses on the design of AI agents which proactively speak to people and how proactive agent speech impacts people's ongoing tasks. My current research project, the ARTICULATE project, examines how educational speech technology can be made more engaging for informal audiences in languages other than English.</p>
